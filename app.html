The first visit downloads Python with NumPy, SciPy, pandas and matplotlib (about 30 MB). Your browser keeps it, so later visits start faster. Everything runs on your computer; your data is never uploaded.

Welcome to Serpentine

You're looking at example data. Here's how it works:

  1. Analysis: pick what you want to find out, in the column on the left.
  2. Data: paste a table from Excel or a LabChart Data Pad, or upload a file.
  3. Settings: choose the statistics, baseline and windows.
  4. Style: make the figure your own. Or just type what you want in the box below, like “remove gridlines”.

Not sure which analysis? Press Help me choose. New in 1.0: Repeated measures, ROC curves, Method comparison and Import a plate for plate readers. Under every result, Report makes a printable summary and Share link sends a colleague the whole analysis.

Press + Board on figures to combine them into one multi-panel figure, and Sign in to save projects online and open them anywhere. Undo (⌘Z) covers everything, and ? shows every keyboard shortcut.

Everything runs on your computer. Projects you save online are end-to-end encrypted, so only you can read them.
